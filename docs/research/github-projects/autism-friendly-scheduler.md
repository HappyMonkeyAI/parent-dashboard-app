## What it does

React app for autistic children to follow daily routines with visual schedules and countdown timers. Schedule dashboard with progress bar, "Up Next" section, circular countdown timer with start/pause/reset, success celebration on completion.

## Why it matters to us

UI patterns specifically designed for autistic users: "Up Next" highlighting, circular countdown timer, progress bar. Simple but the interaction patterns are directly relevant to the child dashboard.

## Cherry-pick (UI patterns, not code)

- "Up Next" highlighting — shows the immediate next task prominently, reducing overwhelm
- Circular countdown timer with visual progress — concrete time representation
- Progress bar showing overall day completion
- Success celebration on task completion — positive reinforcement
- Large colourful icons per activity with clear simple navigation

## Avoid / Gaps

- Very simple (no backend, no parent controls, no multi-child)
- Hardcoded schedule, not configurable by parents
- No license — design inspiration only
- Designed for younger children; needs adaptation for a teen
